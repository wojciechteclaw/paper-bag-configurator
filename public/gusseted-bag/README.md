# Demo artwork of the gusseted bag (torba fałdowa)

The DEMO button with "Torba fałdowa" selected loads the gusseted bag 150 + 60 × 250 mm with one whole-bag (WRAP)
artwork layer (list: `DEMO_CONFIGURATIONS.FOLDED` in `src/ui/demo/demoConfiguration.ts`). Missing files are skipped.

| File | Use | Area | Aspect ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gussted.webp` (5040 × 3000 px) | whole bag, 1:1, shifted left by F = 60 mm (starts at the left gusset) | 2W + 2F = 420 × H 250 mm | 1.68 : 1 |
